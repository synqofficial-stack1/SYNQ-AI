### Full description (≤4000)

> Synq AI gives a small business a team of AI employees that help with sales and marketing, with you approving everything that leaves the app.
>
> **Ask your AI chief of staff.** Chat with an assistant that knows your workspace: how many contacts you have, which email duties are running, who replied. Attach PDFs or photos and get a plain summary with the numbers that matter.
>
> **Keep your sales in one place.** Import contacts from a spreadsheet, keep notes and deals, set targets, and plan meetings with your team.
>
> **Email outreach you approve.** Connect your Gmail, write an email duty or let the AI draft it, review every message, and approve before anything is sent. Synq then shows who replied and summarises the replies.
>
> **Marketing help.** Brainstorm campaigns, captions and reel ideas with the marketing AI, and create images and posters for your posts.
>
> **Work as a team.** Invite colleagues into your company workspace, chat, and assign tasks. The owner approves who joins.
>
> Synq uses a daily allowance of free AI credits that refreshes every 24 hours. Paid plans are not available yet.
>
> You can delete your account and its data at any time from Settings, or from the web.

## Data safety form

Collected data is sent to Synq's servers and service providers (Supabase, Google Gemini/Veo, Pollinations). Play counts transfers to service providers, and transfers the user starts themselves (sending an approved email through their Gmail, publishing an approved post), as **not shared**, so every row below is "Shared: No".

| Data type | Collected | Required? | Purposes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → Name | Yes | Required | Account management, App functionality |
| Personal info → Email address | Yes | Required | Account management, App functionality |
| Personal info → User IDs | Yes | Required | Account management |

| Photos and videos | Yes (uploads, generated media) | Optional | App functionality |

Security practices: data is encrypted in transit (**Yes**); users can request deletion (**Yes**, in app and on the web).

## Release tracks

1. **Internal testing** first: upload the first AAB by hand in Play Console. Google's API cannot create an app's first release, so `eas submit` only works from the second build onwards.
2. **Closed testing**: a *personal* developer account created after November 2023 must keep at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row before Google allows production. An *organization* account (needs a D-U-N-S number) is exempt.
3. **Production** after Google approves the production access request.
